### 🏴‍☠️ APT Deep Persistence & Dark Web IP Auction
An advanced persistent threat (APT) campaign focused on sub-OS hardware persistence and high-value corporate intellectual property extortion.

* **Firmware & UEFI Implants:** Threat actors flash custom, malicious option ROMs and UEFI DXE drivers directly into motherboard SPI flash memory. These rootkits survive complete operating system reinstalls and hard drive wipes.
* **Kernel-Level Hypervisor Evasion:** The malware executes before the operating system kernel boots, allowing the attacker to intercept hypervisor hypercalls and tamper with virtualization-based security (VBS).
* **Proprietary IP Staging:** Automated background threads identify and exfiltrate proprietary source code, algorithmic patents, unreleased product roadmaps, and merger documentation.
* **Darknet Auction Launch:** The stolen trade secrets are published on an encrypted onion marketplace, offering exclusive buy-it-now rights to state-sponsored actors and competitor syndicates.
* **Strategic Enterprise Risk:** Permanent loss of corporate competitive advantage, catastrophic devaluation of trade secrets, and ongoing supply chain liability.
