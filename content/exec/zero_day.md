### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Public Web Server CVE Exploit
A zero-day Common Vulnerabilities & Exposures (CVE) exploit has compromised public application servers. The Board must decide whether to take public storefronts offline for emergency patching or maintain customer access.

**Executive Governance Directives:**
* **✅ Approve IT Recommendation (Full Containment Directive):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Short-term selloff due to offline checkout portals, but market rewards decisive vulnerability elimination &bull; Reputation: Public frustration over portal blackout, but shields payment backends from catastrophic exposure.*  
  Authorizes IT to take public gateways offline for a 24-hour emergency patch cycle. Sacrifices immediate online revenue, but provides a high likelihood of completely eradicating the remote code execution flaw.
* **⚖️ Downgrade to Business Compromise (Balanced):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Cautious market response; questions remain on whether custom firewall rules completely stop intrusion &bull; Reputation: Intermittent false-positive blocks on customer logins; moderate brand friction.*  
  Directs IT to deploy Web Application Firewall (WAF) virtual patching while keeping portals largely active (6-hour maintenance window). Preserves sales, but provides moderate containment likelihood as encoded payloads may bypass inspection filters.
* **🚫 Overrule IT Plan (Protect Business Continuity):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Preserves immediate checkout revenue, but poses existential downside risk if attackers seize customer databases &bull; Reputation: Temporary customer ignorance; catastrophic brand collapse upon eventual public disclosure.*  
  Orders portals to stay online with zero downtime. Protects quarterly financial metrics today, but leaves servers open with low likelihood of stopping active attacker exploitation.
