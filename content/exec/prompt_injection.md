### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: AI LLM Prompt Injection & API Pivot

#### 1. Strategic Incident Assessment
Adversaries have executed adversarial prompt injection attacks against CloudStore's customer-facing AI shopping assistants. By bypassing safety guardrails, threat actors have forced the LLM to expose internal API keys, database connection strings, and backend service credentials.

#### 2. SEC Materiality & Regulatory Exposure
* **Proprietary AI Liability & Compliance**: Unchecked LLM prompt injection leading to backend data leakage triggers immediate FTC scrutiny under consumer protection and deceptive trade practice statutes.
* **Customer Trust & Brand Erosion**: Weaponizing customer-facing AI erodes market confidence in CloudStore's enterprise technology roadmap, directly threatening brand valuation and enterprise partnerships.
* **SEC 8-K Disclosure Assessment**: If exposed API keys allow unauthorized exfiltration of corporate financial or customer data, mandatory federal disclosure obligations are triggered.

#### 3. Boardroom Governance Mandate
The Board of Directors must deliberate on the **technical containment plan formulated by the IT Department** (summarized below).
