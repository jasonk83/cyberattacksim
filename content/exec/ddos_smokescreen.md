### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: DDoS Smokescreen

#### 1. Strategic Incident Assessment
A high-volume distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) bombardment is saturating CloudStore's public edge networks, disrupting e-commerce checkouts and customer-facing web services. Forensic triage indicates this massive volumetric attack may be a deliberate diversion designed to distract security teams while deeper intrusion or data staging occurs behind the scenes.

#### 2. SEC Materiality & Commercial Impact
* **Direct Revenue Attrition**: Public storefront unavailability causes immediate transaction leakage, estimated in hundreds of thousands of dollars per hour during peak shopping periods.
* **Reputational Churn**: Frustrated consumers are airing grievances across social platforms, damaging customer lifetime value and brand sentiment.
* **Secondary Intrusion Risk**: Treating this solely as an availability glitch leaves core databases and internal identity providers vulnerable to undetected exfiltration under the cover of the traffic flood.

#### 3. Boardroom Governance Mandate
The Board of Directors must review the **containment recommendation submitted by the IT Department** (detailed below). The Board must balance the commercial imperative of rapid storefront restoration against the technical requirement to isolate and defend internal networks from surreptitious lateral breaches.
