### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: DDoS Smokescreen
A massive distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) flood is saturating CloudStore's public edge networks, disrupting checkouts while potentially masking surreptitious lateral data staging behind the scenes.

**SEC Materiality & Commercial Impact:**
* **Direct Revenue Attrition**: Public storefront downtime causes immediate revenue leakage, estimated in hundreds of thousands of dollars per hour during peak commerce windows.
* **Reputational Churn**: Frustrated consumers venting across social channels rapidly damages brand sentiment and lifetime customer value.
* **Secondary Intrusion Hazard**: Treating volumetric floods solely as an availability glitch leaves internal databases vulnerable to undetected exfiltration under the cover of the traffic noise.

**Boardroom Governance Mandate:**
The Board of Directors must review the **containment recommendation submitted by the IT Department** (detailed below). The Board must balance the commercial imperative of rapid storefront restoration against the technical necessity of isolating internal networks from hidden intrusion vectors.
