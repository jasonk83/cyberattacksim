### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Production Database Sabotage & Deletion
Core corporate financial and shipping records have been sabotaged. The Board faces an acute dilemma between shutting down commerce to rebuild trustworthy data or operating with compromised records.

**Executive Governance Directives:**
* **✅ Approve IT Recommendation (Full Containment Directive):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Severe short-term stock devaluation during total shutdown; restores fundamental enterprise valuation upon verified rebuild &bull; Reputation: Severe operational freeze sparks widespread frustration, but guarantees data integrity and prevents corrupt orders.*  
  Authorizes an emergency 36-hour total shutdown to restore clean, uncorrupted cold backups. Causes immense short-term revenue loss, but offers a high likelihood of complete data integrity and disaster prevention.
* **⚖️ Downgrade to Business Compromise (Balanced):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Analysts maintain watchful wait-and-see rating; questions linger on inventory accuracy &bull; Reputation: Sporadic order glitches and checkout delays; moderate negative sentiment.*  
  Directs IT to institute an 8-hour partial outage to audit tables line-by-line while keeping checkout available. Moderates immediate financial impact, but delivers moderate mitigation likelihood as subtle table corruptions may persist.
* **🚫 Overrule IT Plan (Protect Business Continuity):**  
  *Stock Outlook: Temporary stock stability, followed by sudden collapse if corrupted transaction logs cause restatements &bull; Reputation: Customers encounter phantom inventory and unfulfilled orders, causing widespread customer abandonment.*  
  Demands normal operations continue with zero downtime. Protects quarterly financial reporting today, but provides low likelihood of containment as corrupted records multiply errors and trigger SEC audits.
