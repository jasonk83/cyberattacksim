### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Production Database Sabotage & Deletion
Core corporate financial and shipping records have been sabotaged. The Board faces an acute dilemma between shutting down commerce to rebuild trustworthy data or operating with compromised records.

**Executive Governance Directives:**
* **✅ Approve IT Recommendation (Full Containment):**  
  Authorizes an emergency 36-hour total shutdown to restore clean, uncorrupted cold backups. Causes immense short-term revenue loss, but guarantees complete data integrity and prevents downstream legal chaos.
* **⚖️ Downgrade to Business Compromise (Balanced):**  
  Directs IT to institute an 8-hour partial outage to audit tables line-by-line while keeping checkout available. Mitigates financial impact, but leaves 50% of the corrupted data uncorrected.
* **🚫 Overrule IT Plan (Protect Business Continuity):**  
  Demands normal operations continue with zero downtime. Protects quarterly financial reporting, but customer orders will scramble and false financials risk severe SEC sanctions.
