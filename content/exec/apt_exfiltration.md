### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Mass Customer PII & Card Exfiltration
An Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) syndicate is exfiltrating millions of customer transaction ledgers, hashed credentials, and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to foreign command-and-control servers, presenting CloudStore's most severe regulatory and brand crisis.

**SEC Materiality & Regulatory Exposure:**
* **Mandatory SEC Materiality**: Mass exfiltration of customer records constitutes an unambiguous material event under SEC rules, necessitating formal Form 8-K disclosure within 96 hours.
* **Global Regulatory Enforcement**: European GDPR regulations mandate up to 4% of annual global turnover in administrative penalties; state CCPA and FTC actions carry severe statutory damage claims.
* **Capital Markets Contagion**: Breaches involving payment card data trigger catastrophic equity downgrades, merchant transaction processing surcharges, and institutional divestment.

**Boardroom Governance Mandate:**
The Board of Directors must review the **containment recommendation issued by the IT Department** (summarized below). The Board must decide whether to authorize emergency database outages and token wipes to halt exfiltration, negotiate a partial maintenance compromise, or overrule IT to maintain commerce.
