### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Mass Customer PII & Card Exfiltration
A severe regulatory and corporate brand crisis. An Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) group is exfiltrating Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and payment credentials. SEC disclosure requirements and FTC privacy penalties loom.

**Executive Governance Directives:**
* **✅ Approve IT Recommendation (Full Containment):**  
  Authorizes an emergency 36-hour database outage and credential revocation. Inflicts immediate customer checkout downtime, but halts exfiltration and proves proactive fiduciary responsibility to regulators.
* **⚖️ Downgrade to Business Compromise (Balanced):**  
  Authorizes an 8-hour partial maintenance outage to restrict outbound data ports. Moderates revenue loss, but reduces technical threat mitigation by 50%.
* **🚫 Overrule IT Plan (Protect Business Continuity):**  
  Rejects database outages to prevent stock panic and public discovery. Zero immediate downtime, but risks catastrophic regulatory fines when stolen data appears on the dark web.
