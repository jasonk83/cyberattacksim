### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Spear Phishing & Credential Compromise
Adversaries compromised internal employee credentials via spear phishing. The Board of Directors must weigh immediate operational disruption against enterprise data security and SEC regulatory liability.

**Executive Governance Directives:**
* **✅ Approve IT Recommendation (Full Containment):**  
  Authorizes IT to execute an emergency 24-hour company-wide lockout and workstation reset. This inflicts severe business disruption and frustrates employees, but guarantees 90% breach containment and eliminates lateral movement.
* **⚖️ Downgrade to Business Compromise (Balanced):**  
  Directs IT to cut downtime to a 6-hour targeted isolation of suspect finance workstations. Preserves general corporate productivity, but halves technical protection (50% containment loss).
* **🚫 Overrule IT Plan (Protect Business Continuity):**  
  Rejects workstation lockouts to prevent employee panic and stock speculation. Zero operational downtime, but leaves the compromised credentials active for attackers to escalate privileges.
