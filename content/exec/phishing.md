### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Spear Phishing & Credential Compromise
Threat actors have breached CloudStore's corporate perimeter using targeted spear phishing against internal employees, harvesting administrative credentials and session tokens to access internal management portals.

**SEC Materiality & Regulatory Exposure:**
* **SEC 4-Day Disclosure (Form 8-K)**: If administrative credentials permit access to customer financial records or core transaction systems, mandatory disclosure is triggered within four business days.
* **Customer PII & Class-Action Liability**: Compromised corporate accounts frequently serve as staging grounds for mass customer data harvesting, exposing the firm to FTC enforcement and shareholder derivative lawsuits.
* **Capital Markets Sentiment**: Wall Street rewards swift, decisive containment while heavily penalizing corporate concealment or repeated breach escalation.

**Boardroom Governance Mandate:**
