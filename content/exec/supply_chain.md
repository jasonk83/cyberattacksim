### 💼 Executive Crisis Briefing: Supply Chain Pivot
A compromised third-party software library has introduced unauthorized backdoors into CloudStore's platform, allowing threat actors to use CloudStore's digital certificates and client integrations as a trusted pivot to infiltrate downstream partner networks.

**SEC Materiality & Regulatory Exposure:**
* **B2B Contractual Liability & Indemnification**: Downstream enterprise clients facing breaches are invoking contract breaches, indemnification covenants, and service-level damage claims.
* **SEC Cyber Risk Oversight**: Failure to contain vendor-originated vulnerabilities exposes the board to shareholder derivative lawsuits alleging deficient cybersecurity oversight.
* **Commercial Partner Trust**: Enterprise client churn threatens high-margin recurring revenue (ARR) and institutional investor confidence.

**Boardroom Governance Mandate:**
The Board of Directors must review the **containment recommendation submitted by the IT Department** (detailed below). The Board must balance the business friction of revoking vendor certificates against the existential legal liability of spreading breaches across client networks.
