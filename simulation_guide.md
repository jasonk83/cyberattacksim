# Instructor & Student Guide: Cyber Attack Simulation

Welcome to the Cyber Attack Simulation. This exercise is designed to help first-year undergraduate IT students understand the dynamics of a cyber breach from three distinct perspectives. 

## The Three Roles

During the simulation, participants will take on the following roles:

### 1. The Hackers (Red Team)
The Hackers initiate the breach. Their goal is to identify vulnerabilities and execute attacks that compromise the target organization's data, operations, and finances. 
* **Focus:** Maximizing disruption and financial gain.
* **Decisions:** Choosing attack vectors ranging from simple phishing emails to complex ransomware deployments.

### 2. The IT Security Team (Blue Team)
The IT team represents the first line of defense. Their goal is to detect the hackers, stop the spread of the attack, and restore systems safely. 
* **Focus:** Mitigating technical damage and restoring operations.
* **Decisions:** Choosing defense strategies like isolating networks, wiping hard drives, or hunting for hidden threats.

### 3. The Executive Team (Leadership)
The Executive team handles the business fallout of the breach. Their goal is to protect the company's reputation, manage legal risks, and ensure the business survives.
* **Focus:** Public relations, legal compliance, and financial survival.
* **Decisions:** Choosing whether to apologize publicly, hire outside legal help, or secretly pay a ransom.

## How the Simulation Works

Participants will be presented with scenarios and must choose an action. Each choice comes with a **Projected Impact**, showing how many points it is expected to add to (or subtract from) the company's overall health score.

**Wait, "Projected" Impact?**
Yes. Just like in the real world, things do not always go according to plan. The actual result of your choices will be calculated dynamically based on the complexity of the action you took. Be prepared to adapt!
